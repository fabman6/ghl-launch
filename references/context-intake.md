# Context Intake Checklist

Harvest before asking. For each item: check memory, connected tools (Drive proposals, Gmail threads, Fireflies kickoff calls, the GHL location itself), and the client's public web presence. Interview only for what remains.

## Tier 1: Required before any build (minimum viable context)

| Item | Why it gates the build | Typical source |
|---|---|---|
| Legal/brand business name, DBA | Naming everything, A2P consistency | Proposal, website |
| Industry/vertical + core offer(s) | Pipeline design, workflow selection, copy | Proposal, kickoff call |
| Target audience (who buys, who books) | Copy voice, form fields, calendar design | Kickoff call, website |
| Primary lead sources (ads, GBP, referrals, forms, calls) | Which triggers/workflows matter first | Kickoff call |
| Booking model (calls? in-person? classes? none) | Calendars, reminders, no-show flows | Kickoff call |
| Brand voice (2 or 3 adjectives + a sample) | All messaging copy | Website, socials, brand doc |
| Owner's email + notification recipients | Notifications, deliverables | Anywhere |
| Timezone + business hours | Calendars, send windows | GHL location, website |

## Tier 2: Strongly wanted (build is better with these)

- Pricing posture (publish prices or quote-based)
- Existing contact list to import (source, size, consent status; consent gates SMS)
- Website/domain situation (existing site, new funnel, or both; who controls DNS)
- Phone situation (existing number to port/track vs new; A2P readiness: EIN, address, sample messages)
- Review profiles (Google Business Profile link, current rating)
- Social accounts to connect (which platforms, who has creds)
- Payment stack (Stripe connected? invoicing needs?)
- Team members and roles (who gets user seats, who is assigned leads)
- Competitors the client respects or hates (positioning fuel)
- Existing assets (logos, photos, testimonials, old email copy)

## Tier 3: Nice to have

- Seasonality and promo calendar
- Compliance constraints (regulated industries: legal, medical, finance change copy rules)
- KPI targets (what number makes month one a win)

## Interview discipline

- Batch the ask: one message with the short list of missing Tier 1 items, not a drip of questions.
- Offer defaults: "I'll assume Mon to Fri 9 to 5 in your timezone unless you say otherwise" converts unknowns into confirmations.
- Record what you learn (memory/notes) so the next launch for this agency starts warmer.
- If the user says "just use your best judgment," take it: fill with industry-standard defaults, mark each assumption in the blueprint, and keep moving.
